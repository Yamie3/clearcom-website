Core capabilities

Ownership

Every issue and action is assigned to a responsible owner with a defined timeline — accountability is explicit.

Tracking

Progress, updates, and status changes are tracked in one transparent system without email chasing.

Evidence

Supporting documents and commentary remain linked to each action, preserving a complete audit trail.

Oversight

Dashboards give leadership real-time visibility into what is open, overdue, and closed.
